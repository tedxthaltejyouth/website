Office of the Registrar

Verify a document

Every letter, certificate, ID and sealed page issued by TEDxThaltej Youth carries a code. Enter it to check that it is genuine and still valid.

You can also enter the register number (TXTY/REG/2027/0001) or a seal code (S-XXXX-01). Scanning the QR code on a document opens this page with the code filled in.

Letters

Recommendation, association and appointment letters, and official notices, on our letterhead.

Certificates

Appreciation, participation, completion, awards and side events, signed by the Organizer & Licensee.

Seals & IDs

Membership IDs (VID, E-VID) and physical documents stamped with numbered seal codes.
