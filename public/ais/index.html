01 · Every city

Every city quietly produces ideas.

In classrooms, laboratories, design studios, workshops, hospitals, libraries, neighbourhoods, startups and countless everyday conversations.

02 · Unseen

Most ideas are not lost because they lack significance.

They remain unseen because no one is actively looking for them.

03 · Few mechanisms

Cities produce ideas continuously. Very few have ways of discovering them.

The responsibility of a platform that serves a city is to listen for them.

So we set out across Amdavad to do exactly that.

What AIS is

Let us begin with what it is not.

It is not

an event.

It is not

a competition.

It is not

a speaker recruitment programme.

It is

A year-round idea discovery and development framework.

Conceptualised by TEDxThaltej Youth to continuously engage with the intellectual, cultural, social and innovative life of the community it serves: the city of Ahmedabad.

From speaker search to idea discovery

It begins with a different question.

The usual question

Who should speak on our stage?

Our question

What ideas are emerging within the communities we serve, and who is exploring them?

An idea may exist long before its author considers themselves a speaker: in a student’s question, a teacher’s experiment, a researcher’s discovery or a family’s lived experience.

Listening to Amdavad

A city of many doorways.

Like the carved doors of the old city’s pols, every community in Ahmedabad opens onto its own world of ideas. AIS knocks on all of them, so that TEDxThaltej Youth never depends only on the voices that are easiest to find.

2017

Ahmedabad became India’s first UNESCO World Heritage City. Its ideas have always lived in its pols, courtyards and conversations. We are going to where they already are.

Nurturing before the stage

Like a stepwell, every step goes deeper.

Many meaningful ideas are still developing when they are first encountered. AIS creates space for them to mature through dialogue, research, reflection and constructive feedback. The objective is not to manufacture polished speakers, but to understand whether an idea has genuine substance, relevance and originality.

Not every discovery will become a TEDx talk.In fact, most will not.That is intentional.
The Idea Steward

Helping the city see its own ideas.

An Idea Steward is an individual or institution that helps identify and bring attention to meaningful ideas emerging within its own community. Through this network, AIS reaches communities that TEDxThaltej Youth might otherwise never reach.

A school

Helps identify students or educators exploring questions worth pursuing.

A university

Brings emerging research and interdisciplinary thinking into the Search.

A community organisation

Helps surface lived experiences and local solutions.

A research institution

Helps identify discoveries that could contribute to broader public understanding.

Idea Stewards do not select TEDx speakers.
They help make ideas visible.

Language should be a bridge to an idea, not a barrier to discovering it.

A person’s ability to articulate an idea in English should not decide whether it deserves to be discovered. AIS welcomes conversations in Gujarati, Hindi and English, in whichever language people can express themselves most honestly.

Protecting both processes

Four roles. Kept distinct.

AISdiscovers.
Developmentnurtures.
TEDx curationselects.
The TEDx stageamplifies.

Discovery through AIS does not constitute selection for a TEDx talk. Ideas identified through the Search remain subject to the independent curation, review and TEDx requirements that apply to TEDxThaltej Youth.

The vision

A simple, meaningful shift.

From
Applications→Selection→Stage
To
Listening→Discovery→Development→Curation→Stage

A TEDx event should not ask a community to come to its stage once a year. It should spend the year going into the community, listening to what is already being said, and discovering what deserves to be heard.

A community before a stage

Our aspiration is not to build an event that could happen anywhere.

It is to build an event that could only happen here.

Not because of its venue, but because it is shaped by the people, the institutions, the cultures and the conversations that make Ahmedabad unlike any other city. A stage should never be the beginning of a conversation. It should be the place where conversations already growing within a community are finally given a voice.

Ahmedabad Idea Search

The year-round framework

The framework through which ideas across the city are discovered and developed.

A TEDx event takes place on a particular day. Community discovery cannot. The annual event becomes one moment within a much longer process of listening.

The measure of success

Not how many. How well we listen.

01

Whether ideas that would once have remained invisible are discovered.

02

Whether relationships are built before speakers are sought.

03

Whether the communities of Ahmedabad recognise themselves in the ideas on our stage.

04

Whether unheard voices feel that this stage belongs to them.
