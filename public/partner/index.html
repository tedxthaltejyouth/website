TEDx partnership rules

Recognition, not promotion.

As a TED licensee, every partnership follows TEDx rules. They keep the stage about ideas, which is exactly what gives your association its value.

  1. No selling or promotion from the stage. Partner logos appear on stage only while partners are being thanked.
  2. Partners have no say in which speakers are chosen or what they talk about.
  3. In talk videos, partner logos sit on one static slide at the start and end, smaller than our event logo.
  4. Social content must be about the event. Only TEDxThaltej Youth team members appear on our social media.
  5. Some industries cannot sponsor TEDx events under TED’s prohibited sponsors list.
