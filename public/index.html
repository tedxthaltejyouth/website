TEDxThaltej Youth is a youth-led TEDx event from Thaltej, Ahmedabad: a stage built by young people for the ideas their city is quietly producing, with live heritage performances and a year-round search for ideas.

In the spirit of ideas worth spreading, TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events. This independent TEDx event is operated under license from TED.
