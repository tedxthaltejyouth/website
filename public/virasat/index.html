The name

What Virasat Means

Virasat
noun · a common word across Indian languages
  1. Heritage.
  2. Legacy — something kept, and carried forward.

Virasat means heritage — something kept, and carried forward. This is where TEDxThaltej Youth shares its cultural heritage with the world, alongside its ideas.

Pressed by hand

Heritage, block by block

Like a block print, heritage is made one careful impression at a time — pressed, repeated and carried forward until it becomes a pattern a whole community recognises.

Our purpose

Why Virasat Exists

To give our local heritage and art forms a bigger stage.

What we think

TEDxThaltej Youth was built around ideas worth spreading.

What we keep

But a community is not only what it thinks. It is also what it keeps, what it celebrates and what it passes on.

What we share

Our cultural heritage is among the richest things we have to offer.

Virasat exists to share that heritage with the world, alongside our ideas: a dedicated space with its own team and its own standard, so that the art forms of our community are not an afterthought between talks, but a full part of the stage.

Two kinds of work

What We Do

On the stage

Performances take their place in the middle of our events. The best performers we can find, with music that is entirely their own.

Beyond the stage

Culture is also the bonds we build — with our speakers, and within our own team. Virasat brings the team together, not only the audience.
